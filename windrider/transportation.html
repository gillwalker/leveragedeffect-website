The Geography

Three close stops, one long hop.

The hotel, the church, and the regional bus stop are close enough to walk between in a pinch. That's what makes the shuttle loop efficient — one short local hop, then everyone funnels onto the same Boulder-bound route.

  • 0.4 mi
    Hotel ↔ Ascent Church2-minute drive, walkable in good weather
  • 0.7 mi
    Ascent Church ↔ US36 & McCaslinDirectly across McCaslin Blvd from the church
  • 0.6 mi
    US36 & McCaslin ↔ HotelCloses the loop — the third leg of the triangle
  • ~18 min
    McCaslin ↔ Downtown BoulderBy charter, US36 corridor, ~7–8 miles
  • ~30 min
    McCaslin ↔ Boulder via Flatiron FlyerRTD regional route, every 10–15 min at peak

The Daily Rhythm

Five waves of films, two waves of demand.

Sundance hasn't released its 2027 Boulder program yet, but Park City's program has run on a consistent rhythm for years — four to five screening blocks a day, with two clear surges. The shuttle plan is built around those surges, not the quiet hours.

WAVE 01

11:30–1:30

Highest pressure of the day

The hotel shuttle switches to its triangle loop (hotel/Ascent/RTD) right at 11:30 — just as class lets out — feeding the RTD stop and absorbing the first surge at no extra cost.

WAVE 02

1:30–4:30

Steady afternoon flow

The hotel shuttle's triangle loop is still running (until 5:30), continuing to connect the hotel, church, and RTD stop for mixed outbound/return traffic.

WAVE 03

4:30–7:30

Prime premiere slot

The festival's marquee block — dinner plus a film. Generates the evening's biggest return surge afterward.

WAVE 04

7:30–midnight

Smaller, self-selecting crowd

Late and "Midnight" series screenings. One smaller bus on a loose loop, rideshare for stragglers after 1:30am.

For Attendees

How to actually get around, in plain terms.

01

Morning — ride the hotel van to class

The hotel shuttle runs continuous loops from the lobby to Ascent Community Church before 8am. It's a two-minute hop — no need to rush, just don't miss the last loop.

Included with lodging
02

After class — ride the triangle loop to the RTD stop

From 11:30am, the hotel shuttle runs a continuous loop between the hotel, Ascent Church, and the US36 & McCaslin RTD stop — right where you catch the Flatiron Flyer into Boulder.

Included · Triangle loop
03

In Boulder — the HOP bus is free during the festival

Boulder's HOP circulator runs along nearly every Sundance venue and is expected to be free for the duration of the festival. Download the Transit app for live arrival times.

Free in-festival
04

Heading back mid-afternoon or early evening

Until 5:30pm, the hotel shuttle's triangle loop carries you between the RTD stop, Ascent, and the hotel. After 5pm, a chartered bus runs a direct evening loop from Pearl Street through about 10pm.

Triangle loop + Chartered evening bus
05

Late night — a smaller loop, then rideshare

From 8:30pm to about 1:30am, a smaller shuttle loops every 30–40 minutes. After that, Uber or Lyft covers the last stretch — a ride credit pool will be available, roughly $25–40 per trip.

Chartered + rideshare
06

Driving yourself? Park once, ride the rest

Skip downtown Boulder parking entirely. Leave your car at the US36 & McCaslin Park-n-Ride — right across from Ascent Church — and use the same shuttle and transit network as everyone else. Arrive early; the lot fills by mid-morning.

Free RTD lot
Still Confirming

What's locked, what's still open.

This plan is built on the most reliable information available as of mid-2026. A few details depend on Sundance and the City of Boulder finalizing their own plans closer to January 2027 — we'll update this page as those firm up.

  • Whether RTD's regional Flatiron Flyer fare (currently ~$3) gets the same festival-period treatment as Boulder's free HOP bus.
  • HOP's extended late-night hours during the festival — our late-night charter schedule should complement, not duplicate, that service.
  • Sundance's actual 2027 venue and screening schedule, expected late 2026 — will let us refine the "wave" timing against real program data.
  • Whether to subsidize RTD fares for students directly or rely on the chartered loop and free HOP to cover essentially all trips.
